### Turning day-to-day factory data into systems and reports people use every day.

</div>

I'm an **MIS Data Analyst at Omex Gears** (Dharam Power Transmission Equipments Pvt. Ltd.), a B2B gearbox manufacturer. I build business systems on **Google Sheets and Apps Script** (order tracking, inventory, purchase, BOM, maintenance) and keep the data behind our MIS reports clean. Before data, I worked in a **pharma QC lab** (HPLC, GMP documentation), which taught me that a decision is only as good as the data behind it. Outside work I practise **SQL (MySQL, PostgreSQL)**, build **Power BI** dashboards and am learning **Python**.
